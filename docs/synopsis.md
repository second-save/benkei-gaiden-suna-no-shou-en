# Synopsis - *Benkei Gaiden: Suna no Shou* (*Chapter of the Sands*)

Spoilers enclosed — translators and reviewers read this so early Act 1 lines never accidentally leak Act 2 reveals, and callbacks across chapters align accurately.

## Historical Premise
- Over eighty years after Minamoto no Yoshitsune and Musashibo Benkei were said to have perished at Hiraizumi, rumors linger that Yoshitsune escaped across the sea and helped forge the Mongol Empire alongside Genghis Khan.
- **1274 (Bun'ei Campaign):** Kublai Khan, grandson of Genghis Khan and ruler of the Great Yuan Empire, launches 900 warships and 33,000 soldiers against northern Kyushu. A sudden storm obliterates the invasion fleet.
- Perceiving that an ancient Japanese mystic art conjured the tempest (the *Storm-Calling Rite*), Kublai Khan dispatches **Jugon**, first of the **Four Realm Generals** leading the **Curse Realm Sect**, to hunt down Japan's Mystics and seize the source of that divine power.
- Regent **Hojo Tokimune** of Kamakura summons mystics across the land to defend Japan. Yet Jugon's sect overwhelms them, slaughtering defenders across the realm.

## Act 1: Japan (1276, Two Years After the Bun'ei Campaign)
1. **Shumon Fort & Tragedy:** The protagonist **Fudo** (customizable name & gender; male or female orphan of the *Suna* bloodline) trains under adoptive master **Saizo** at Shumon Fort in Shikoku. **Hayate** of the Tendo Clan arrives under Imperial summons, urging Saizo to report to Kyoto. Before Fudo leaves, **Rakan** seals the fort inside the **Happo Outer Array** barrier; Rakan reveals only that Fudo carries the blood of "the Sands" (*Suna*). Fudo breaks the barrier (`Rakan` boss fight), only to return and find Saizo slain by **Jugon**, leaving Fudo alone with a fragment of truth to seek out.
2. **Allies & The Hunt for the Rings:** Traveling out through Tosa Village, Fudo meets **Mikage** (calm, disciplined jutsu adept) and **Tokoyo** (brash, loyal swordsman), survivors of Nagato's ruined Renbukan dojo who were told to seek Saizo. Learning from the monk **Shogen** on Sado Island of Fudo's heritage, and passing the trials of **Genzo**'s **Kasumi Clan** (joined for a time by the kunoichi **Shinobu** and aided by Kasumi officers **Hiei** and **Kagemaru**, with Hiei later dying in the line of duty), the party visits Lord **Yoshiyuki Ogata** in Tanabe and Regent **Tokimune** in Kamakura.
3. **Awakening Benkei:** As Shuko Pavilion's barrier protecting Japan's Storm-Calling Rite comes under assault, Hayate makes a desperate rear-guard stand inside **Mt. Tsurugi** to buy time, unsealing the **War Demon's Coffin**. From centuries of slumber rises **Musashibo Benkei** — jovial, thunderous, and unstoppable. Benkei rescues the grievously wounded Hayate, delivers him to a healer, and joins the youths.
4. **Kikai Castle & Departure for China:** Using the five **Spirit Crystal Rings** and the **Sea Dragon Bone**, the party raids Jugon's Kyushu stronghold **Kikai Castle**. Jugon brags that his subordinates have already unearthed the ancient **Divine Beast Mirror** and shipped it back to the Yuan mainland before Fudo and Benkei strike him down in the Curse Realm. Too late to save **Shuko Pavilion** — whose collapse costs Japan the *Storm-Calling Rite* and opens the way for Kublai Khan's massive second armada (142,000 troops, 4,400 ships) — the heroes take custody of **Meisa**, an amnesiac girl connected to Yoshitsune's final message. Honoring Yoshitsune's call to stop the spreading darkness, Fudo, Mikage, Tokoyo, and Benkei sail with Meisa for mainland China to seek the mythical sanctuary of **Kunlun**.

## Act 2: Mainland China, Silk Road & The Four Realms (ca. 1281)
1. **Changsha & Byakuren:** Shipwrecked and washed ashore near **Changsha Village**, Fudo is rescued by a young boy named **Byakuren** and his mother. Despite lacking combat strength, Byakuren insists on guiding and traveling alongside the party as their brave companion.
2. **The Silk Road Trial:** Moving through **Shaolin Temple**, **Bodhidharma Cave**, **Luoyang**, **Xi'an** (`Genyo Tower`), **Datong** (`Yungang Grottoes`), **Lin'an**, **Dadu** (`Mongol fortifications` and **Wall Ruins**), **Lanzhou**, **Dunhuang**, **Urumqi**, and **Hotan**, the party crosses swords with Curse Realm Sect commanders (**Bukoku** of the Demon-Beast Realm, **Jako** of the Illusion Realm, and continental bosses **Li Daiguan**, **Seiran**, **Senmei**, **Master Jukei**, **Shigaraki**). Along the way, **Chizam** aids them — until Jako murders Chizam off-screen and impersonates him to trap the party in the **Illusion Realm** and separate Fudo from the others inside the **Ryukoku Subterranean Castle**.
3. **Byakuren's Sacrifice:** Inside Ryukoku Realm, **Byakuren** risks everything to seize the **Ring of Ruin** (*Shihourin*) from Jako's grasp, tossing it to break the barrier and suffering a fatal blow. He dies in the party's arms calling out for his mother — though **Rakaku**, Fudo's conflicted Suna counterpart on the enemy side, silently restores Byakuren's life at the climax so the boy returns safely to his mother in Changsha (`1D_9DA9`).
4. **Kunlun & Ancient Tsushima:** Gathering the **Crescent Flute** and **Soaring Seal**, the heroes reach the hidden paradise of **Kunlun**. There **Gosha**, Queen Mother of the West, greets Meisa by her true name — **Reika** — revealing Reika was Kunlun's twin-chosen envoy sent to the mortal world. Through the **Twin Sal Trees** and **Ancient Tsushima**, the party witnesses the millennium-old flashpoint where Queen **Himiko** unsealed the **Divine Beast Mirror** to summon the sacred **Kirin**, warning that true terror would awaken a thousand years later inside the **Magic Palace**.
5. **The Magic Palace & Sogyoku:** Passing through the realms within **Magic Palace** (*Curse*, *Demon-Beast*, *Illusion*, *Ryukoku*), Fudo and friends slay **Sogyoku** — who mocks them that he *is* the living will of the Magic Palace itself, the dark mirror image of Kunlun — and reclaim the four ultimate sacred blades (**Dragonscale Koryu**, **Seio Shion**, **Taishaku Suzaku**, and **Reiho**).
6. **Kublai Khan & The Emperor of the Four Realms:** Atop the throne room of Tsushima's Magic Palace, **Kublai Khan** declares that just as the heroes inherited Yoshitsune's will, he bears Genghis Khan's blood and will finish their ninety-year duel by drawing the **Emperor of the Four Realms** into his own flesh. The heroes defeat the resurrected **Emperor of the Four Realms**.
7. **Collapse & Epilogue:** As Magic Palace crumbles, a cataclysmic tempest breaks over Tsushima, sinking Kublai Khan's 4,400 ships and 142,000 troops in the sea. **Benkei** braces the collapsing chamber so the young heroes escape, laughing defiantly that he will crawl back even from the depths of Hell. On Kunlun, Reika/Meisa chooses mortal happiness with the party over eternal paradise before Kunlun seals its gates for another thousand years.
   - **Epilogues:** Meisa lives in **Dunhuang** with Reika's delighted parents; **Mikage** wanders Japan praying for the war dead before succeeding Genzo as head of the **Kasumi Clan**; **Tokoyo** seals his sword and reopens **Renbukan** as a school for war orphans, eventually joined by **Shinobu**.
   - **Protagonist Fate:** **Fudo** returns to Shikoku's ruined **Shumon Fort** before departing across the continent again (male Fudo sets off into the horizon on a nameless wandering quest; female Fudo weeps at Saizo's grave, feeling the karma of the *Suna* fade, and sets out dressed warmly as an ordinary young woman). No longer did they ever all gather in one place again — bringing one hidden chapter of history to a close.

## Spoiler Guardrails for Translators
- Never call **Meisa** *"Reika"* before Gosha addresses her as Reika in Kunlun (`1D_9EF5` / `1C_F611` reveal); before that, even Amnesiac-Meisa hints only at Dunhuang and lost memories.
- Keep **Chizam** loyal and genuine in his early scenes — do not tip off the player that **Jako** murders and impersonates him until `1D_91F3`.
- Treat **Minamoto no Yoshitsune**'s survival and link to Genghis Khan as rumor/legend in Act 1 NPC chatter until confirmed late in Act 1 (`1B_FA99`, `1B_FE49`) and Act 2 (`1D_9550`).
- Keep **Rakan** and **Rakaku** distinct: Rakan is the early Shumon barrier boss who later leaves the Golden Sword *Koryu*; Rakaku is the enigmatic Suna figure tied to the enemy who ultimately saves Byakuren.
